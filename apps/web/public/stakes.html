Stakes

Put your tokens into a stake and a share of the pool's trading fees is yours, paid in ETH. Deposit one coin and OrdoFi swaps, pairs and stakes it for you in a single transaction. OrdoFi takes 1% of the fees a stake earns, never from your deposit.

Create a stake

Attach a stake to your token's ETH pool so holders can deposit and earn a share of trading fees, paid in ETH. The pool must already exist on Uniswap V3. Anyone can create one, once per pool; there is no owner afterwards.
