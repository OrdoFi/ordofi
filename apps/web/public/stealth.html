    01 · STEALTH MODE

    A fresh address every time

    With the toggle on, your browser derives a brand-new address from the recipient's published keys. Only they can spend from it. Nothing on-chain connects it to their wallet.

    02 · ONE TRANSACTION

    Announce and deliver atomically

    OrdoStealthSend publishes the breadcrumb the recipient needs and delivers the funds in the same call. Either both happen or neither does. The contract holds nothing and has no admin.

    03 · THEY JUST SEE IT

    Found with a signature

    The recipient's browser scans announcements with a viewing key derived from their wallet. Matches are theirs; everything else is noise. Withdrawals are signed locally and sent through OrdoFi's own RPC.

    What stealth mode hides, and what it can't. It hides the recipient: the address that receives is never linked to their wallet. It does not hide the sender — your wallet pays for the transaction and that is public, like every transaction on this chain. Hiding a sender means pooling or relaying funds through someone else's, and that is a mixer; we don't run one. The recipient has to turn private receiving on once before anyone can pay them this way; the 0.25% fee applies only in stealth mode.
